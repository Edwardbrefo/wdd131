WEBSITE PROJECT PROPOSAL
========================

SUBJECT
Club Pulse Ghana

CONTENT
A website for Ghanaian football fans to find club information, upcoming matches, photos, and connect with other fans.

Key Pages:
- Home page with upcoming matches
- Club profiles and team information
- Match calendar and events
- Photo gallery of stadiums and clubs
- Fan signup and community section

Colors: Gold, Red, Green, Black
Fonts: Oswald (headings), Inter (body text)

REASON
I chose Club Pulse Ghana because:

1. It's about something I care about - Ghanaian football culture
2. It serves a real community need
3. It lets me practice different web skills (forms, galleries, calendars, JavaScript)
4. It's relevant to Ghana and shows local pride
5. It will be a strong portfolio project

This project combines my passion for football with web development skills.
